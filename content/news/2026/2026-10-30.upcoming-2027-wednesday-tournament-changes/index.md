---
title: Upcoming 2027 Wednesday Tournament Changes
---

Starting January 1st, blitz/rapid rated tournaments will now be 9 rounds, while standard tournaments will remain 7 rounds.Effective January 1, 2027, our Wednesday Classical Tournaments will be expanded to four sections. The top two sections will be both CFC-rated and FIDE-rated, providing more opportunities for players to earn official ratings and compete at a higher level.

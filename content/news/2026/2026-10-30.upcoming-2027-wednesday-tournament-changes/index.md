---
title: Upcoming 2027 Wednesday Tournament Changes
---

Starting January 1, 2027, our Wednesday Tournaments are expanding to four sections: Open, U1700, U1400, and U1100.

To give players more opportunities to compete at a higher level and earn official ratings, both the Open and U1700 sections will be CFC- and FIDE-rated.

Section Placement: Players will be placed into sections based on their highest published CFC or FIDE rating prior to the start of the tournament.
